## Favicon

`favicon/favicon-master.png` is the transparent 1024 px source. PNG exports are
provided at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px, together with `favicon.ico`.

These assets were generated as one coordinated direction: graphite, cool white,
and a restrained signal-blue accent, built around the idea of speech emerging
from shadow.
